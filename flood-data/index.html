Flood & recovery01

NFIP redacted claims

FEMA OpenFEMA

Redacted administrative claims from the National Flood Insurance Program, describing insured flood losses. These are claims against NFIP coverage, not a complete inventory of all flood-affected homes.

Use with care. Insured claimed losses, not all damage or flood-event counts. Redacted geography is not a verified building location. Confirm current API version and retain original extract vintage.

NFIP redacted policies

FEMA OpenFEMA

Redacted policy and term records from the National Flood Insurance Program. Used to study insurance coverage and construct measures of uptake after explicit record and time-window handling.

Use with care. Overlapping terms are not unique insured homes; renewals can yield two terms. Do not silently replace study extract with current data.

03 / Responsible reuse

Three checks before you combine.

Reproducibility depends on the choices between the original download and the final table.

01 · Match space and time

Record study area, tract or parcel vintage, source years, map effective dates and coordinate reference system. Flag missing coverage instead of treating it as zero.

02 · Verify the meaning

Keep units, denominators, uncertainty and table universes. A count is not a rate; an insurance record is not all damage; a projection is not an observation.

03 · Preserve provenance

Save the provider, version, retrieval date, license and processing steps. Public availability does not automatically permit every use or redistribution of a derived package.
